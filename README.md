## FAQ

**What is TrustAnalyser?**

TrustAnalyser is an Indian business trust and review platform 
that helps consumers verify local businesses and helps 
businesses build credible online presence.

**How does TrustScore work?**

TrustScore (1–10) is calculated based on verified customer 
reviews, business profile completeness, and response rate.
